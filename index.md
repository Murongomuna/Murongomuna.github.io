## Featured work

### Life Assurance Policy Retention Analysis

**Bachelor's dissertation | Survival analysis and machine learning**

- Investigated a 17.3% life-insurance policy lapse ratio in Zimbabwe.
- Distinguished mid-term cancellations from end-of-term non-renewals.
- Used a Weibull accelerated failure time model to estimate time to policy lapse.
- Built a CatBoost model that achieved 83.19% accuracy and 45.72% precision.
- Demonstrated how predictive analytics can support customer-retention decisions.

**Methods:** Survival analysis, Weibull AFT, CatBoost, predictive modelling

_Public case study and selected analysis materials are being prepared._

### Roja Scout Web Platform

**Sole creator and product owner | Applied AI exploration | Property-search marketplace**

Roja Scout is my independently created, Zimbabwe-focused property platform. It is designed to help tenants find rental accommodation and connect directly with landlords and agents. I took the project from an identified market problem to a live digital product, using multiple generative-AI systems to accelerate learning, test ideas, solve technical problems, and explore how AI-assisted property discovery could work in practice.

Key areas explored:

- Property-search and filtering workflows
- AI-assisted property discovery
- Listing quality, verification, and community reporting
- Direct and private communication between users
- Housing requests and property matching
- Mobile-first access to rental-market information

**AI-assisted workflow:** ChatGPT, Claude Sonnet, Gemini 3.1 Pro, and DeepSeek

This project demonstrates independent problem framing, product ownership, experimentation with unfamiliar technology, and the ability to translate an idea into a working service. It complements my core data-analysis profile rather than replacing it.

[Visit the Roja Scout website](https://www.rojascout.co.zw)
